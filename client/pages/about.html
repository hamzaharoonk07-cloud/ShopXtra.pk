Our story

Self-care, one essential at a time

ShopXtra started from a simple observation: the products that matter most in a day aren't one category, they're a rhythm. A hydrating drink before you've had coffee. A bar of soap before the day gets away from you. A shade of lipstick before you leave the house in the evening. Four categories, used at different hours, that had never been sold together until now.

We source electrolytes, coffee, shampoo, and cosmetics that share one idea: premium shouldn't mean complicated. Soft, considered, and priced for daily use across Pakistan.

ShopXtra is built with the support of dedicated sourcing, quality, and operations teams. Every product goes through careful review before it's listed, because trust is earned, and once given, it must never be broken.

ShopXtra is a lifestyle brand, created for people who value quality, honesty, and refinement in the products they choose. We believe true elegance comes from consistency and care, not exaggerated promises.

We focus on:

  • Transparency: honest claims, no false promises
  • Quality: carefully selected products and vetted suppliers
  • Trust: long-term results over short-term hype
  • Refinement: simple, elegant, and purposeful design
  • Responsibility: products made with care for people and daily routines
